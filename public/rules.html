QUIPSMASH!

How to play in four steps

Teams of 5 3 rounds ~15 minutes Play on your phone
1

Get in

Scan the QR code on the big screen, or go to the web address shown there. Type the 4-letter code and your name.

You'll be put on a team of five. Watch the screen to see who's with you.

2

Write one line

A prompt appears on your phone — something like "The most dangerous sentence in a project status deck…" Type the funniest answer you can.

80 characters. Short and punchy beats long and clever.

3

Your team picks

You'll see every line your team wrote, with no names on them. Vote for the best one — that's the one your team sends to the big screen.

You can't vote for your own. Captains break ties.

4

Judge the rest

Your team's line goes head to head against another team's. Everyone not in that matchup votes for the funnier one.

You never vote on your own team's answer. Just enjoy it.

How points work

Vote sharePoints are split by how many votes your line got, so a 40–10 win pays far better than a 26–24 squeak.
×1 ×2 ×3Each round is worth more than the last. The grand final is the top four teams only — it's still anyone's game going in.
Solo awardWhoever's own lines earn the most points across the night takes the sharpest quip award.
Clean sweep: take every single vote in a matchup and your team gets a 25% bonus and a badge on the big screen.

Good to know

  • 👀Keep it work-appropriate. The host reads every answer before it's projected and can pull anything. Your name is next to it.
  • ⏱️Miss the timer and a placeholder is submitted for you. It is deliberately not funny.
  • 📶Lost signal or locked your phone? Just reload the page — you keep your team, your score and anything you'd typed.
  • 🕵️Nobody knows whose line is whose while people are voting. Your team and your name only appear once the votes are in.
  • ⚑See something off? Tap the flag beside any answer to report it straight to the host.
  • 🏳️Team captain? You'll see an extra button to lock your team's answer in early, and your vote settles a tie.
